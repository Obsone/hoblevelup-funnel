Questions

Questions Before You Book.

Do I need to be experienced to book an assessment?

No. I'll use the assessment to understand your starting point, including if structured training is completely new to you.

Is the assessment a sales call?

No pressure. It's a practical conversation about your goals and options. I'll only discuss ongoing coaching if it genuinely fits what you need.

Can I train around a busy schedule?

Yes. We'll use the assessment to identify a training rhythm that works with the time you can realistically protect.
